Take Your Career to the Next Level
Data science is expected to remain among the fastest growing fields, with applications expanding across academia, government, healthcare, industry, and the nonprofit sector3. As demand for skilled practitioners increases, the online MSDS program at UT Austin offers a rigorous pathway to build both the technical capacity and the statistical credibility that modern data roles require. Our program provides the complete foundation you need to thrive in today's complex data environments. You'll master computational tools, scalable algorithms, and automation techniques, while developing rigorous standards of statistical practice, ensuring that your insights meet the evidentiary standards executives, regulators, and stakeholders trust.

Course: Advanced Predictive Models (DSC 383)

Review 1

Rating: 4
Difficulty: 4
Professor: 5
Workload: 5 hours per week

Pros:
- Cool material not seen elsewhere
- Easy A
- Professors are active on Ed discussion boards

Cons:
- Lots of self-study required
- Course organization was poor
- Assignment expectations were unclear

Detailed Review:
The reviewer felt the course had interesting material and helpful professors. However, the class required significant self-study. Communication on discussion boards was sometimes slow and there were not enough teaching assistants available. Assignments included both peer-graded and staff-graded work.

Review 2

Rating: 2
Difficulty: 1
Workload: 2 hours per week

Pros:
- Easy A

Cons:
- First half of the course felt useless
- Too many manual calculations
- Quiz grading was frustrating

Detailed Review:
The reviewer felt the course did not teach enough predictive modeling. They believed too much time was spent on spatial modeling and matrix calculations that were already covered elsewhere in the program. The reviewer recommended redesigning the course.

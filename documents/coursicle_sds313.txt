Course: SDS 313 - Elementary Statistical Methods

Course Description:

Introduction to statistical reasoning and data analysis.

Topics:

- Descriptive statistics
- Probability
- Hypothesis testing
- Confidence intervals
- Regression basics
- Statistical inference

Student Notes:

Students report that the course provides a strong foundation in statistics. Understanding probability concepts is important for success. Regular practice with statistical problems improves performance.

Skills Developed:

- Data interpretation
- Statistical analysis
- Quantitative reasoning
- Basic regression analysis
